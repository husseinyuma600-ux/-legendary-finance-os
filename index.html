Manual savings controls

Calculate automatically, then decide how much you actually move. You can also record extra savings or set the existing balance.

For money already in NMB Savings, use “Set existing balance” and enter it as the account’s opening balance. Do not record it as income or as a new transfer. For money moved today, record a transfer from the source account to the savings account. The app keeps the automatic 10% estimate separate from actual transfers so the same money is not counted twice.

Savings rules

Automatic calculations are allocations, not proof that money physically moved.

*This is an indicative gap, not proof of missing money. Actual transfers may include savings funded from older balances. Opening balances are excluded from transfer totals.
